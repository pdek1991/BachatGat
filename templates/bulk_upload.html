Upload a CSV file to add multiple monthly contributions or loan repayments at once.

Download Sample CSV Format

CSV Requirements:

  • Must contain headers: `user_name`, `type`, `amount`, `date`
  • Optional headers: `is_cash_payment` (0 or 1), `is_paid` (0 or 1), `loan_id` (required for loan repayments)
  • `type` column can be `contribution` or `loan_repayment`.
  • `date` format should be YYYY-MM-DD.
  • For loan repayments, `loan_id` must be the ID of the specific loan.
